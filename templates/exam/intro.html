Security & Proctored Rules
  • Fullscreen Enforcement: The exam will lock into full-screen mode. You cannot start the exam without enabling fullscreen, and exiting fullscreen records a violation.
  • Window Focus and Tab Switches: Moving away from this window (e.g. switching tabs, launching another application, or clicking secondary screens) records a security violation.
  • Browser Shortcuts Blocked: Right-clicking, copy-paste mechanisms, view-source (Ctrl+U), inspect element (F12, Ctrl+Shift+I) are programmatically blocked.
  • Auto-Submit Threshold: If you accumulate {{ 1 }} violations, your exam attempt will be terminated and submitted immediately.
  • Server-side Clock: The exam has a strict time limit of {{ exam_config.exam_duration }} minutes. If the timer expires, the exam submits automatically.
Question Navigation: Questions are presented one at a time. You must answer the current question to advance. You cannot go back or skip questions.
